## Purpose

This register documents the provenance, eligibility, and access limitations of official guidance reviewed for the five-CVE pilot. The registration unit is one document version associated with one CVE. Document-level coding and CVE-level synthesis will retain these source identifiers.

## Recording Conventions

Source identifiers follow the sequence S001, S002, and onward. The same document version retains its identifier across associated CVEs; different reviewed versions receive distinct identifiers. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD. Missing publication or update dates are recorded as “Not stated”; unchecked dates are recorded as “Not verified.”

Publisher types are Vendor, Government, and CSIRT. Source eligibility is recorded as Pending, Include, or Exclude, with a rationale. Access status is recorded independently as Accessible, Partially accessible, Inaccessible, or Superseded. Only eligible, accessible content supports coding. Inaccessible material is retained as a retrieval limitation and is not treated as evidence that guidance is absent.

Document relationships are recorded as CVE-specific, Multi-CVE with relevant coverage, Explicitly referenced generic guidance, or Unclear. Linked guidance is evaluated within the direct-link boundaries established by the protocol. Official status alone does not establish relevance to a CVE.

Retrieval status is recorded as Not started, In progress, or Complete within defined boundaries. Completion refers to the documented source-discovery procedure and does not establish that no other guidance exists. Post-cutoff updates and unavailable historical versions are recorded explicitly.

The register contains no applicability judgments, actionability scores, evidence-specificity scores, or empirical findings. Those records will be maintained separately under the existing coding guide.
