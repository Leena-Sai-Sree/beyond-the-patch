## Applicability

| Code | Classification | Numerical scoring |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Applicable | Assign both scores independently |
| A2 | Not applicable, with documented justification | NA |
| A3 | Conditional | Record the trigger and its status; unresolved conditions receive NA |
| A4 | Undetermined | NA; record the missing information |

Applicability concerns the defined CVE scenario and is assessed independently of whether a document discusses the category. Source silence alone does not establish nonapplicability.

For A3 records with an established trigger, document the evidence establishing the trigger and score only the guidance relevant to that condition. Conditional findings remain identifiable in synthesis.

## Independent Scoring Scales

| Score | Operational actionability | Evidence specificity |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | No relevant action identified | No relevant evidence requirement identified |
| 1 | General action or concept | General reference to evidence or verification |
| 2 | Specific steps or procedures | Identifies artifacts, observations, or evidence sources |
| 3 | Detailed reproducible procedure with relevant parameters or sequence | Identifies evidence and explains its use in verification or decision-making |

The two scores measure separate dimensions. Evidence specificity of 3 does not establish that the evidence proves absence of compromise.

## First Pilot Case: CVE-2024-3400

**Analysis date:** 2026-10-28  
**Coding status:** 70 first-pass document-category records and seven synthesis records; human review pending  
**Source inventory and version evidence:** [Pilot Source Register](source.md#pan-os-case-source-review)

### Analytical Scenario P3400-A

This is a document-analysis scenario, not an observed incident: a self-managed, internet-exposed VM-Series firewall running an affected PAN-OS 11.1 release, with GlobalProtect enabled, and credible evidence of interactive root command execution and possible configuration disclosure before remediation. It is managed locally, without HA, Panorama, or Strata Cloud Manager. Credential replacement, containment and recovery are therefore relevant in this scenario. Key-encryption guidance is restricted to compatible 11.1 deployments.

All seven categories are A1 for this scenario, independently of source coverage. Conditional response triggers are assumed within the analytical scenario only; no incident facts have been established. These scores must not be applied unchanged to an exposed device with no evidence of exploitation, or to a hardware appliance. Case-level E4/E5 applicability for those other scenarios could be A3 or A4.

The scenario makes a pilot operational choice explicit; it is not a frozen main-study rule. Its severity can affect coverage and must be considered before cross-CVE comparisons. No sources were executed against an asset. No human time records, independent agreement, or supervisor approval are claimed.

### Category Boundaries Used

- E3 includes restricting further attack access with interface protection as well as isolation; E6 may separately cover verification of that mitigation. Overlap is recorded, not summed.
- E4 includes protection and replacement of secrets. Encryption checks verify one configuration property; they do not demonstrate revocation or replacement of every exposed secret or session.
- E5 covers rebuilding, safe restoration, integrity assessment and the asset-decommission gate. Those actions do not automatically earn E7 credit.
- E7 covers explicit advice supporting a response disposition, including the vendor article's FAQ about assessing the device after a hotfix. Its limited E7 credit does not imply a complete administrative closure standard.
- A source's references do not inherit the scores of linked documents. Each included document is coded separately.
