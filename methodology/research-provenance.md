# Research provenance

## Preparation
AI assistance was used to draft and edit project documentation, summarize public sources, prepare first-pass PAN-OS, MOVEit and Outlook coding, and develop the repository validation script. The coding records are analytical judgments about published guidance, not measurements from production incidents.

## Researcher review
Source review covered the 70 PAN-OS and 84 MOVEit records. Specific Outlook interpretations were also reviewed, as recorded in the review log. Original first-pass records and later accepted decisions are kept separately so that revisions can be followed. This review does not constitute an independently produced second-coder dataset.

## Data and automated output
The KEV snapshot and registered source metadata identify the documentary inputs. The CSV and JSON files are data records. Validation reports are generated by the script and describe checks of record structure, source references, scores, summaries and local links. They do not substitute for source interpretation or technical testing.

## Editorial revisions
The November 9 editorial pass replaced questionnaire shorthand in the narrative notes with source-based reasoning, shortened repetitive passages and clarified historical versus current status. It did not change the original 175 coding records, accepted score revisions or historical source dates. Original assistance labels remain in the historical coding records.
